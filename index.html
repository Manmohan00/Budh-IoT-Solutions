How we work

From "what if" to something that actually runs.

01

Share your problem

A rough idea, a business problem, or a half-built prototype — all valid starting points.

02

Feasibility check

We tell you honestly what's realistic, what it'll take, and what to expect — before any build starts.

03

Design & prototype

Hardware selection, architecture, and a working proof-of-concept you can see and test.

04

Build & test

From prototype to something field-ready — tested under the conditions it'll actually face.

05

Deploy & support

Rollout, handover, and support after launch — not disappearing the day it ships.

What we offer

We're not selling a product.
We're solving your problem.

What we build, guaranteed — plus anything else IoT-shaped you bring us. Pricing is scoped per project; let's talk through what you need.

STEM & IoT Education

Hands-on IoT and STEM programs for schools, colleges and summer camps — the kind where every student leaves with something they actually built, not just slides and a certificate.

  • School & college workshops
  • Summer camps
  • Internships
  • Structured courses

Smart Scale Models

Bringing architectural, engineering and exhibition scale models to life — IoT-controlled lighting, motion and actuators that turn a static model into something that moves, lights up and responds.

  • Automated lighting
  • Actuators & motion
  • Interactive triggers

Industrial Monitoring & Automation

Already running on real production floors — counting output, tracking process parameters, flagging anomalies before they turn into downtime. We instrument equipment and processes you already have, not a replacement for them.

  • Production & count monitoring
  • Process sensing
  • Predictive alerts
  • Retrofit automation

Smart Retail & Space IoT

Instrumented counters, showrooms and commercial spaces — footfall and occupancy sensing, smart displays, environment monitoring — fitted into a space that's already working without disrupting it.

  • Footfall & occupancy sensing
  • Smart displays & shelving
  • Environment monitoring

Rapid Prototyping & Product Development

Got something IoT-shaped that doesn't fit a category yet? From a rough idea to a working prototype — sensors, connectivity, firmware, dashboards — this is how we find out what's possible before committing to a full build.

  • Rapid prototyping
  • Feasibility & PoC
  • Full builds on request
